Privacy & how this works

Simple, calm, and completely transparent about your data.

Stuck detection On

When turned Off, the stuck detector and gentle check-in stop completely (no state changes, no cat movement, no bubbles).

1

We only measure the time since your last key or mouse activity, and how often you switch windows. We never read what you type.

2

Window and app names are checked on your device only and are never saved or sent.

3

The text you type in the tiny-step box, your cat energy level, and a matching tip are sent to the AI (Gemini).

4

If you use the microphone button, your voice recording is sent to Groq to turn it into text.

5

If you connect Google Classroom, your assignments stay on this device.

6

Nothing is sold or shared. You can turn tracking off at any time.

Settings are stored locally on your computer.
